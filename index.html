A Letter For You 🖤

I've been thinking about writing this for a while. I started typing it so many times, only to delete everything because I wasn't sure if I was ready. But I guess some feelings deserve to be said, even if they're scary.

I like you.

Not because you're perfect, but because being around you feels easy. Talking to you, laughing with you, even the smallest interactions somehow became the favorite parts of my day.

I don't expect you to feel the same way, and I'm not writing this to pressure you into giving me an answer. I just wanted to be honest with you instead of wondering, "What if I had told you?"

If you don't feel the same, that's completely okay. I hope this confession doesn't make things awkward between us. I just wanted you to know that someone genuinely admires you, cares about you, and wishes nothing but happiness for you.

And if, by any chance, you feel even a little curious about what this could become... I'd love the chance to get to know you better.

Thank you for reading this. No matter what happens after today, I'm glad I finally found the courage to tell you.

With sincerity,

Fortz 🖤
